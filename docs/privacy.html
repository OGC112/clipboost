Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mint handles information when you use the desktop application and connect supported social platforms.

1. Information Mint may process

Local project data

Mint may process media files, transcripts, captions, project settings, campaign information, exports and editing metadata that you choose to use in the application.

Connected account information

When you connect a supported platform, Mint may receive OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, account/channel identifiers, profile information and the permissions you granted.

Social performance data

Where permitted by the connected platform, Mint may retrieve information such as post or video identifiers, titles, publication dates, thumbnails, views, likes, comments, and other metrics made available by the platform API.

2. How information is used

Mint uses this information to provide features you request, including account connection, publishing, video editing, campaign organization, analytics dashboards, metric synchronization and account-status display.

3. Local storage and OAuth credentials

Mint is designed primarily as a desktop application. OAuth credentials are stored locally on your computer and, where supported, protected using the operating system's secure credential storage. Mint does not display your OAuth secrets back to you in the application.

Project files and other local application data may also remain on your device until you remove them.

4. Third-party APIs

When you connect a service such as YouTube or TikTok, Mint communicates with that service's APIs. Information sent to or received from those services is also governed by the privacy policy and terms of the relevant provider.

5. YouTube API Services

Mint may use YouTube API Services to access information associated with the YouTube account you explicitly connect. Depending on the permissions you approve, Mint may retrieve your channel information, uploaded videos and video statistics, and may upload content when you request publication.

By using YouTube features in Mint, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and acknowledge Google's Privacy Policy.

You can revoke Mint's access to your Google account at any time through Google's account security permissions page.

6. TikTok and other connected platforms

If you connect TikTok or another supported platform, Mint processes only the information and permissions made available through the scopes that you approve. You may revoke access using the platform's account settings or by disconnecting the account in Mint.

7. Sharing of information

Mint does not sell your personal information. Information is shared with connected platforms only when necessary to perform an action you request, such as authentication, synchronization or publishing.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or necessary to protect the security and integrity of the application.

8. Data retention

Local project data remains on your device according to your use of Mint. OAuth credentials remain stored until you disconnect the relevant account, clear the application data, or uninstall/remove the stored data. Third-party platforms may retain information independently under their own policies.

9. Your choices

  • You can choose not to connect any social account.
  • You can disconnect a linked account from Mint.
  • You can revoke platform authorization from the platform provider's own account settings.
  • You can delete local Mint project and application data from your computer.

10. Security

Mint uses reasonable technical safeguards appropriate for a local desktop application, including encrypted operating-system credential storage where available. No system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's privacy

Mint is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Users are responsible for meeting the minimum age and eligibility requirements of any connected platform.

12. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated as Mint changes or as third-party platform requirements evolve. The “Last updated” date above indicates the latest revision.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official support/contact channel associated with the Mint application or the project repository from which you obtained Mint.

Disconnecting a platform

Disconnecting a platform in Mint removes Mint's stored authorization for that account. For additional control, you can also revoke Mint from the connected platform's own security settings.
