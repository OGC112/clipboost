Mint 21.12.0 — Hierarchy Redesign

This is a structural redesign, not a typography/color pass.

Hierarchy changes:
- Home: content/discovery first, campaign rail second; old KPI dashboard hierarchy removed.
- AI Studio: source/editor workspace on the left, preview + AI tools in one right rail.
- Campaign Studio: campaign selector + Smart Import in a left sidebar; selected campaign workspace on the right.
- Library: Sources/Creators sidebar on the left, media discovery grid on the right.
- Projects: filters above a compact two-column project grid.
- Results: performance chart + top clips become the primary row; campaign table follows below.

Existing DOM nodes are moved instead of replaced where possible, preserving the app's native handlers.
Navigation still uses the original internal data-page handlers, with analytics hash fallback.

No backend, APIs, AI engine, campaign logic, Smart Import, Canto, updater or Auto Publisher changes.
No .env or secrets included.
