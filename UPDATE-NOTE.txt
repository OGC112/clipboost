Mint 21.14.3 — Product Navigation Split

Mint is now presented as two clear workflows.

CAMPAIGNS
- Discover: campaign search/import + campaign information
- Campaign Studio: campaign editing workspace
- Results: campaign performance

CREATE
- AI Studio: long-form to short-form editing / own video creation
- Library: creator content discovery
- Projects: non-campaign creative projects

Other changes
- Settings is removed from the page menu.
- Settings is now a gear icon at the top right.
- Home remains the global entry point.
- Added a dedicated Campaign Discover page.
- Campaign cards can open directly into Campaign Studio.

No backend changes.
No AI engine, Smart Import, Canto, updater or Auto Publisher changes.
No .env or secrets included.
