# ClipBoost 21.3.0 — Clip Intelligence v2

## Goal
Validate that generated shorts feel self-contained and less repetitive.

## Test videos
Use at least:
- one podcast/interview with long answers,
- one high-energy/gaming or reaction video,
- one video longer than 30 minutes.

## Selection checks
For the first 5-10 generated candidates, verify:
1. The clip does not begin mid-sentence.
2. The opening makes sense without the previous 10 seconds.
3. The clip does not begin on a dangling pronoun/connector unless the hook still makes sense.
4. The ending contains the answer, reveal, punchline, reaction, conclusion or takeaway.
5. The clip does not stop while the speaker is clearly continuing the same thought.
6. Two selected clips should not tell essentially the same story.
7. Highest-scored clips should have both a strong opening and a satisfying ending.
8. Clips normally stay in the 20-55 second range and never exceed 60 seconds.

## Regression checks
- Auto / 5 / 10 / 20 target still works.
- Candidate thumbnails still render.
- Edited preview still renders.
- Export selected clip and Export all still work.
- Quality card still shows Hook / Story / Retention / Clean values.
- Seamless updater remains unchanged from 21.2.5.
